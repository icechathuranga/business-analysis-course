Business Analysis · foundations → practitioner

Become a Business Analyst — the techniques, end to end.

Learn how a BA finds the real problem, elicits and models needs, writes and prioritises requirements, builds a business case, and presents a recommendation that gets approved. Taught from scratch to job-ready, with a Practitioner’s corner in every module, and mapped to the IIBA BABOK® body of knowledge.

New to BA? Start at Module 0. Already practising? Each module’s Practitioner’s corner goes deeper. One running case — Homegrove, a retailer whose boss wants to “rebuild the website” — ties it all together. (Homegrove is fictional.)

The curriculum

14 modules, one real project

Tick a module when you finish it — your progress is saved in this browser. Work in order, or jump to what you need.

Templates

Ready-to-use Word & Excel templates

Every template a working BA needs — download, fill in, reuse. The Excel ones have live formulas.

Stakeholder Register & RACI

Map every stakeholder by power and interest, then set who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed for each activity.

Problem & Root-Cause Worksheet

Turn a vague complaint into a sharp, measured problem statement, then dig to the root cause before anyone proposes a solution.

Elicitation Plan & Interview Guide

Plan who you will talk to and how, and run interviews that surface the real needs.

Business Requirements Document

The anchor document: problem, objectives, scope, business / functional / non-functional requirements, risks and sign-off.

User Story Backlog

Capture needs as user stories with acceptance criteria, MoSCoW priority and estimates.

Prioritisation Matrix

Score requirements by value and effort (the ratio calculates itself) and set MoSCoW priorities.

Business Case & Cost-Benefit

A live cost-benefit model: enter costs and benefits and the payback, ROI and NPV calculate automatically.

Requirements Traceability Matrix

Trace each requirement back to a business need and forward to design and test cases.

Project on a Page (Readout)

Present a recommendation and get a decision on a single page — ideal for a steering committee.
