# The BABOK knowledge-area map

The **BABOK® Guide** (A Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge, v3) is the IIBA's
standard reference for the profession. It organises business analysis into **six knowledge
areas** — groups of related tasks — plus underlying competencies, perspectives, and a set of
about **50 techniques**. This course teaches the techniques in a natural working order and maps
each module to the knowledge areas below, so your learning also supports IIBA certification
study. *(This is an independent study aid, not affiliated with IIBA.)*

## The six knowledge areas

### 1. Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring
How you'll approach the analysis itself. Tasks: *Plan Business Analysis Approach · Plan
Stakeholder Engagement · Plan Business Analysis Governance · Plan Business Analysis Information
Management · Identify Business Analysis Performance Improvements.*
→ **Module 4** (and stakeholder *planning* in Module 3).

### 2. Elicitation and Collaboration
Drawing out needs and keeping people engaged. Tasks: *Prepare for Elicitation · Conduct
Elicitation · Confirm Elicitation Results · Communicate Business Analysis Information · Manage
Stakeholder Collaboration.*
→ **Modules 3, 5 and 10.**

### 3. Requirements Life Cycle Management
Managing requirements from idea to retirement. Tasks: *Trace Requirements · Maintain
Requirements · Prioritize Requirements · Assess Requirements Changes · Approve Requirements.*
→ **Module 8** (and sign-off / *Approve Requirements* in Module 10).

### 4. Strategy Analysis
Understanding the business, the need, and the change. Tasks: *Analyze Current State · Define
Future State · Assess Risks · Define Change Strategy.*
→ **Modules 1, 2 and 9.**

### 5. Requirements Analysis and Design Definition (RADD)
Specifying, modelling and designing the solution. Tasks: *Specify and Model Requirements ·
Verify Requirements · Validate Requirements · Define Requirements Architecture · Define Design
Options · Analyze Potential Value and Recommend Solution.*
→ **Modules 6, 7 and 9.**

### 6. Solution Evaluation
Checking the delivered solution actually delivers. Tasks: *Measure Solution Performance ·
Analyze Performance Measures · Assess Solution Limitations · Assess Enterprise Limitations ·
Recommend Actions to Increase Solution Value.*
→ **Module 11.**

## Also in BABOK
- **Underlying competencies** — the soft and analytical skills a BA draws on (communication,
  analytical thinking, problem-solving, business knowledge, interaction skills, tools).
- **Perspectives** — how business analysis flexes across contexts: **Agile**, Business
  Intelligence, Information Technology, Business Architecture, and Business Process Management.
  (Agile is covered in **Module 12**.)
- **Approaches** — BABOK v3 contrasts **predictive** (plan-first / waterfall) and **adaptive**
  (agile / iterative) ways of working. (Module 4.)

## Certifications (IIBA)
- **ECBA™** — Entry Certificate in Business Analysis (new to the field).
- **CCBA®** — Certification of Capability in Business Analysis (a few years' experience).
- **CBAP®** — Certified Business Analysis Professional (senior / expert).
- **AAC** — Agile Analysis Certification.

This course covers the breadth needed to begin ECBA study and gives practitioners the depth to
build towards CCBA/CBAP, but always do your exam prep from the current official BABOK Guide.
