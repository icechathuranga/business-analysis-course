# Cheat sheet — BABOK at a glance

A one-screen quick card. The **BABOK® Guide** (A Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge, v3)
is the standard reference from the IIBA (International Institute of Business Analysis): six knowledge
areas, about 30 tasks and around 50 techniques. The fuller map, with tasks and the course mapping,
lives in the companion reference, *The BABOK knowledge-area map*.

## The six knowledge areas

- **Business Analysis Planning and Monitoring** — plan and manage how the analysis work itself will be done.
- **Elicitation and Collaboration** — draw out needs and keep stakeholders engaged.
- **Requirements Life Cycle Management** — trace, maintain, prioritise, change-control and approve requirements over time.
- **Strategy Analysis** — understand the current state, define the future state, and shape the change.
- **Requirements Analysis and Design Definition (RADD)** — specify, model, verify and validate requirements, and define design options.
- **Solution Evaluation** — check that the delivered solution actually delivers value.

The areas are not a sequence — they interleave continuously throughout a piece of work. BABOK also adds
**underlying competencies** (the personal skills a BA relies on) and five **perspectives** (Agile,
Business Intelligence, Information Technology, Business Architecture, Business Process Management).

## Predictive versus adaptive

BABOK v3 contrasts two approaches (the v2 wording was plan-driven / change-driven):

- **Predictive** — plan-first, waterfall-style; scope is agreed up front.
- **Adaptive** — agile, iterative; requirements emerge and are reprioritised each cycle.

## IIBA certifications

- **ECBA™** — Entry Certificate in Business Analysis: new to the field.
- **CCBA®** — Certification of Capability in Business Analysis: a few years' experience.
- **CBAP®** — Certified Business Analysis Professional: senior / expert.
- **AAC** — Agile Analysis Certification: a specialist agile credential alongside the core ladder.

Always check the IIBA for current eligibility and exam details, and prepare from the current official
BABOK Guide. This card is an independent study aid, not affiliated with the IIBA.
