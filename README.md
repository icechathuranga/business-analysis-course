# Business Analysis — a practical course (foundations → practitioner)

A free, self-paced course that teaches the **techniques a Business Analyst actually uses**:
how to identify the real business problem, elicit and model needs, write and prioritise
requirements, build a business case, and present a recommendation that gets approved.

It is **dual-level**: every module teaches from scratch to **job-ready**, then adds a
**Practitioner's corner** for experienced BAs who want to go deeper. Techniques are mapped to
the **IIBA BABOK® v3** body of knowledge so the learning also supports ECBA/CCBA/CBAP study.

🌐 **Live site:** https://icechathuranga.github.io/business-analysis-course/
🎧 **Podcast:** every module narrated as an audio episode (≈2 hours total).

> One running case study ties the whole course together: **Homegrove**, a mid-sized online
> retailer whose COO wants to "rebuild the website" to fix late deliveries — the classic trap
> of jumping to a solution before the problem is understood. *Homegrove is fictional; any
> resemblance to a real business is coincidental and all figures are illustrative.*

## The 14 modules
| # | Module | You'll be able to… |
|---|--------|--------------------|
| 00 | What a Business Analyst really does | understand the role, mindset, and the BABOK map |
| 01 | Strategy & business context | read the business with SWOT, PESTLE, Business Model Canvas, Five Forces |
| 02 | Identifying & framing the problem | separate symptom from root cause; write a sharp problem statement |
| 03 | Stakeholders: find, analyse, engage | map stakeholders (power/interest, RACI) and manage resistance |
| 04 | Planning the BA work | choose a predictive or adaptive approach and plan the analysis |
| 05 | Elicitation | run interviews, workshops, observation, surveys and document analysis |
| 06 | Modelling the process & the data | draw as-is/to-be process maps, use cases, DFDs and data models |
| 07 | Specifying & writing requirements | write clear requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria |
| 08 | Prioritising & managing requirements | prioritise (MoSCoW, value/effort) and trace requirements |
| 09 | Options, feasibility & the business case | build a cost-benefit case (ROI, payback, NPV) and recommend |
| 10 | Communicating, presenting & sign-off | present to a steering committee and win approval |
| 11 | Supporting delivery & evaluating the solution | support UAT and prove the benefits landed |
| 12 | Agile business analysis | work as a BA on Scrum/Kanban teams |
| 13 | Capstone | run an end-to-end engagement and build a portfolio piece |

## What's in here
- **`modules/`** — the full written lessons (Markdown).
- **`lessons/`**, **`tools/`**, **`reference/`**, **`podcast.html`**, **`index.html`** — the website.
- **`downloads/`** — ready-to-use **Word & Excel templates** (stakeholder/RACI, BRD, business case
  with live ROI/payback/NPV formulas, prioritisation, user-story backlog, traceability matrix,
  problem/root-cause worksheet, elicitation plan, project-on-a-page).
- **`cheat-sheets/`** — quick pick-the-right-technique references.

## How to use it
Work through the modules in order (~3–4 hrs/week over ~24 weeks, but go at your own pace),
listen to each episode, do the "Try it" exercise on Homegrove **or your own workplace**, and
assemble the capstone deliverables into a portfolio.
